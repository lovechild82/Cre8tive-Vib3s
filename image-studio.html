✂️ Background Remover

Remove the background from any image instantly using free tools. Perfect for product photos, portraits, and stickers.

How to remove backgrounds for free:
1
Upload your image to remove.bg (remove.bg) — it removes backgrounds in seconds, completely free for small images.
2
Alternatively use Canva's Background Remover — open your image in Canva, click Edit Image, then Background Remover.
3
For mobile, use PhotoRoom app (free tier) — takes under 5 seconds and gives professional results.
4
Download your image with transparent background as a PNG file — ready to use on any background.
